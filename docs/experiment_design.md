# Activation intervention experiment design

## Question and population

For customers funded by signup day 3 who have not activated, does an onboarding nudge/incentive increase D7 activation? Eligibility is evaluated before assignment; randomize eligible customers 1:1 to treatment or control, stratified by acquisition channel and signup cohort where operationally feasible.

## Metrics

Primary metric: D7 activation, defined as at least one meaningful transaction in signup days 0-7. Secondary metrics: D30 retention, transaction count, and value. Guardrails: incentive cost, complaints/opt-outs, fraud/abuse indicators, and negative profitability.

## Analysis

Estimate intent-to-treat risk difference (treatment minus control), a 95% confidence interval, and treatment/control denominators. Report absolute lift and business impact, not only a p-value. Pre-specify segment estimates as exploratory unless powered and multiplicity-controlled. Missing outcomes remain visible and are not silently treated as failures.

## Decision rule

Roll out only if the interval and economic scenario support a positive decision after guardrails. A statistically distinguishable but commercially trivial lift does not qualify. Post-rollout monitoring checks dilution, cost, and segment harm.
